How England Helps

Share of people in England who say they gave unpaid help in each way last year.

  • Giving advice19.8%
  • Keeping in touch with someone who finds it hard to get out17.6%
  • Looking after a home or pet for someone who is away11.3%
  • Taking or escorting someone somewhere9.5%
  • Shopping or paying bills for someone8.4%
  • Babysitting or caring for children8.1%

Source: Liquidline Generosity Study. Additional sources: Community and Engagement Survey 2025/26 (Department for Culture, Media and Sport).
